FAQ

Do you take requests?

Yes — plus a do-not-play list. I’ll mix your must-plays into a set that still reads the room.

How early do you set up?

Typically 60–90 minutes before start time so sound check and lighting are done before guests arrive.

Can you MC the wedding timeline?

Absolutely. Grand entrance, dances, speeches, cake, bouquet — I’ll coordinate with your planner or photographer.

What if something fails?

Backup laptop, extra cables, spare speaker path. I don’t leave an event silent.
